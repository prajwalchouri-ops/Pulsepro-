## Brand & Style

The brand persona balances executive-level operational rigor with the frictionless warmth expected of modern premium on-demand lifestyle utilities. It serves two distinct audiences within a unified aesthetic: discerning homeowners demanding vetted, immaculate service delivery, and elite independent service professionals requiring high-density operational telemetry. 

The aesthetic is Modern Tactile Minimalism with soft dimensional depth. It merges crisp SaaS precision—dense data structures, rigorous typographic alignments, and purposeful state indicators—with airy, luminous consumer interfaces. The UI avoids clinical starkness through warm lavender-tinted neutrals, diffuse layered shadows, and rhythmic micro-interactions that evoke reliability, speed, and discretion.

## Colors

The palette employs a deliberate hierarchy engineered around deep purple tonal shifts and high-contrast status feedback:

- **Primary Canvas & Neutrals:** Background surfaces default to soft lavender neutral (`#F7F5FF` to `#FAF8FF`), establishing an immediate departure from sterile stark white. High-tier foreground cards utilize pure white (`#FFFFFF`) to pop against the tinted substrate. Typography relies on deep dark charcoal (`#191526`) for primary readouts and muted violet-gray (`#6E6A82`) for metadata, sub-labels, and structural icons.
- **Brand & Action Layer:** The primary brand anchor is deep purple (`#6C3BFF`), used for primary interaction drivers, active states, and focal iconography. Secondary interactions utilize the violet spectrum (`#8B5CF6` stepping to `#7C3AED`) for subtle directional gradients on conversion elements and primary pill accents.
- **Accents & Badges:** Subtle container fills leverage soft lavender (`#EDE7FF`) to house category tags and inactive chips. 
- **Functional Semantics:** Operational telemetry is strictly encoded: Vibrant Emerald (`#10B981`) denotes real-time provider availability, verified credentialing, and booking confirmations; Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`) is reserved exclusively for ratings and priority alerts; Rose (`#EF4444`) handles critical errors and cancellations.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on Plus Jakarta Sans across all display, body, and UI label roles. The font provides clean geometric precision for administrative metrics and booking schedules, paired with humanist warmth across consumer touchpoints.

- **Headlines & Display:** Tightly tracked headings (-0.015em to -0.03em) maximize visual punch and conserve horizontal real estate across mobile scheduling views.
- **Body & Data:** Open apertures and generous optical x-height ensure effortless scanning of detailed service specs, pricing schedules, and customer reviews.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase or small-caps treatments are restricted exclusively to `label-xs` metadata tags and status indicators; all other interactive UI controls remain in sentence case with tight medium or semi-bold weights.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an 8-point base spatial system configured across three responsive breakpoints:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, 32px margins, 24px gutters. Dashboard content is centered with a max-width container of 1440px to prevent visual sprawl on ultra-wide viewports.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column fluid grid, 24px margins, 16px gutters. Service configuration tables collapse into dual-column cards.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column fluid grid, 16px margins, 16px gutters. Search headers, status badges, and booking drawers span the entire column width with sticky anchored bottom navigation.

Spacing tokens dictate vertical pacing: 4px/8px (`space-xs`/`space-sm`) are strictly reserved for tight sibling relationships (e.g., star icons beside rating counts, form input label spacing); 16px (`space-md`) serves as the standard internal padding for card bodies and input heights; 24px/40px (`space-lg`/`space-xl`) define container segment boundaries and multi-tier section layouts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use harsh drop shadows or stark black overlays. Depth is achieved through tinted ambient elevation and delicate boundary definition:

- **Card Shells:** Raised surfaces employ pure white backgrounds paired with a dual-layer border system: an exterior hairline boundary of `rgba(108, 59, 255, 0.08)` and an ultra-soft ambient shadow tinted with the primary hue: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(108, 59, 255, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(25, 21, 38, 0.03)`.
- **Floating Controls & Modals:** Mobile booking action bars and calendar popovers increase spatial separation with an expanded ambient bloom: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(108, 59, 255, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(25, 21, 38, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive State Transitions:** Resting cards elevate by 2px on hover, shifting their border contrast to `rgba(108, 59, 255, 0.18)` while diffusing the purple-tinted shadow outward.
- **Surface Layering:** Fixed navigation and drawer elements implement a frosted glass blur backdrop (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with `rgba(247, 245, 255, 0.85)` surface opacity, allowing underlying page elements to pass through softly without sacrificing contrast.

## Shapes

The design language favors soft, organic geometry without descending into childlike roundness. The core shape radius is anchored at level 2:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Use `rounded-2xl` (1rem to 1.25rem) to evoke comfort and high craftsmanship.
- **Inputs, Buttons, and Form Fields:** Standardized on `rounded-xl` (0.75rem to 1rem) for an ergonomic, thumb-friendly tap target.
- **Badges, Filters, and Chips:** Full pill contouring (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for category filters, status chips, and avatar verification badges, creating clear visual contrast against the squared structural cards.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#6C3BFF` background (or subtle linear gradient `#8B5CF6` to `#7C3AED`), white text, `label-lg` styling, 48px height, `rounded-xl`. Active state triggers a subtle 0.98 scale transform.
- **Secondary Action:** Pure white fill, 1px border of `rgba(108, 59, 255, 0.16)`, `#6C3BFF` label.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Zero border, muted violet-gray (`#6E6A82`) hoverable text with an instant transition to `#EDE7FF` background on hover.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Pill Badges:** Fully circular ends (`rounded-full`), 24px height, horizontal padding of 10px. 
- **Verified/Available:** Vibrant emerald (`#10B981`) text against an emerald tint background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`), paired with a 6px solid pulsing emerald status dot.
- **Ratings:** Warm amber (`#F59E0B`) icon with solid `#191526` score text on an amber tint container (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`).

### Search & Location Selector
- **Composite Input Bar:** A single pill or `rounded-2xl` container housing a split trigger: Service Search on the left and Geo-Location selector with a pinpoint icon on the right, divided by a soft `rgba(108, 59, 255, 0.12)` vertical hairline. Inactive text reads in muted violet gray (`#6E6A82`).

### Service Cards & Provider Modules
- Pure white container, 1px `rgba(108, 59, 255, 0.08)` border, `rounded-2xl` clipping. Header sections embed provider portrait, emerald verification badge, base hourly rate in high-contrast `title-lg`, and star ratings grouped together.

### Bottom Action Bar & Navigation (Mobile)
- A floating docking bar suspended 16px above the device safe area, surfaced in 85% translucent white with a 16px blur radius. Houses primary booking checkout triggers or quick category filters flanked by circular quick-chat and profile triggers.
